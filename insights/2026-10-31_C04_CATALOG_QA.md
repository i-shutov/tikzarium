## Finding and rationale

`extensions/PACKAGE_MATRIX.csv` contains 14 static family rows, but before
C04 there was no executable gate checking their manifest IDs against the
five bundled TOML declarations and C02's five fixed fixture IDs. A typo or
future optimistic CSV update could silently misrepresent Lua-only support,
package installation, or visual capability. The metadata is now subject to
an independent fail-closed Python 3.11+ structural check; no executable
extensions are loaded.

  **14 tests PASS**, including duplicate IDs, missing manifests and fixtures,

  fields, and symlinked source metadata.

- No local cargo/rustc, xelatex or GUI were available. Rust compiler check,
